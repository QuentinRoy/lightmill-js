`@lightmill/log-api/vocabulary` exports the terms of the API without loading zod or the OpenAPI document: `mediaType`, `atomicMediaType`, `sessionCookieName`, `runStatuses` and `RunStatus`, `userRoles` and `UserRole`, and `httpStatuses` with `HttpStatusMap`, `HttpStatusCode` and `HttpStatusText`.
